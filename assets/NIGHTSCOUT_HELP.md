### Instructions

1. Go to your Nightscout site
2. Go to the Top Right Menu and click "Admin Tools"
3. Under "Subjects - People, Devices" click "Add new Subject"
4. In the popup under "Name" put "carer" and "Roles" put "readable,careportal"
5. click "Save"
6. On the carer row, right click on the blue text in the "Access Token" column and select "Copy Link Address"
7. Paste that address into Carer, it should look like "https://example.com?token=your_token"

### Trouble Shooting

1. If you are using T1Pal, the token will work but you must replace the "Uploader URL" with that from the "Uploaders" settings page for your site.
2. Other hosted services might need a different URL as well.

# Alarms

The types and features of alarms is important

### Dexcom G7
Sound/Vibrate/Match phone settings
Each alert can have individual sound (select from a couple dozen noises)

1. Urgent Low (Level, sound/vibrate, snooze time after acknolwedge)
2. Urgent Low soon in 20 mins ( sound/vibrate, snooze time after acknolwedge) https://ca.provider.dexcom.com/articles/getting-know-urgent-low-soon-alert
3. Low (Level, sound/vibrate, snooze and snooze time to repeat after acknolwedge)
4. High (level, sound/vibrate, delay 1st alert and time, snooze and snooze time to repeat after acknolwedge)
5. Rising fast (rate choice of two 0.1-0.2, or aove 0.2 per minute, level above to alert,  sound vibrate)
6. Falling fast (rate choice of two 0.1-0.2, or aove 0.2 per minute, level below to alert,  sound vibrate)

Other alerts
1. Signal loss (time)
2. Sensor issue for time
3. Technical Alert

Additional featuers
Quiet modes (basically snooze all but maybe still vibrate, something like an exam setting)
Adding second alert profiles
